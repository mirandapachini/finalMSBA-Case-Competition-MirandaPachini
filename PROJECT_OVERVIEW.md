Project Overview
================

Purpose
-------
This repository contains a complete MSBA case competition submission focusing on telecom churn prediction. The goal of this overview is to give future employers a quick orientation to the project's contents, outcomes, and where to look for evidence of technical and business skills.

Key deliverables
----------------
- Notebooks with the analysis and holdout scoring: notebooks/telecom_churn_analysis.ipynb, notebooks/holdout_scoring.ipynb
- Individual write-ups: individual/personal_contribution.md, individual/implementation_strategy.md, individual/deployment_recommendations.md, individual/data_sources_analysis.md
- Team presentation: group/presentation/telecom_churn_presentation.pptx

Highlights
---------
- Final model: CatBoost with strong out-of-sample ROC–AUC
- Business impact: recommended retention strategies for customer segments most at risk of churn
- Reproducibility: notebooks designed to run top-to-bottom in a Jupyter environment

How to evaluate quickly
-----------------------
1. Open the notebook: notebooks/telecom_churn_analysis.ipynb
2. Read the short summary of contribution: individual/personal_contribution.md
3. View the presentation for a concise business-focused summary: group/presentation/telecom_churn_presentation.pptx
